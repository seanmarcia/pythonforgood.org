Sponsor Python for Good

Support Code that Makes a Difference

We're very proud to be organizing the Python for Good event. This gathering, to our knowledge, is the first and currently only conference of this kind for the Python programming language. We are bringing together Pythonistas to learn, teach, and work on projects that directly serve our communities.

Why Sponsor?

By the very nature of our event, our attendees are some of the most passionate, connected, and involved individuals in the Python community. For companies looking to hire, this is an excellent opportunity to meet and interact professionally and socially with prospective candidates.

By sponsoring Python for Good, you demonstrate your commitment to:

  • Supporting communities in need through technology
  • Advancing the Python ecosystem and open source development
  • Fostering a collaborative coding culture focused on social impact
  • Building connections with highly motivated, skilled developers
