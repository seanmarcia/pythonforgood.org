Code of Conduct

Fostering a Respectful, Inclusive Environment

All participants, including attendees, sponsors, speakers, volunteers, and organizers, are required to agree to and follow this Code of Conduct to help ensure a safe and welcoming environment for everyone.

🤝 Respectful Behavior

Treat everyone with respect. Harassment and exclusionary behavior are not acceptable. This includes, but is not limited to:

  • Offensive verbal or written comments
  • Sexual language or imagery
  • Deliberate intimidation, stalking, or following
  • Harassing photography or recording
  • Sustained disruption of talks or other events
  • Inappropriate physical contact or unwelcome sexual attention

🌈 Inclusive Environment

We are committed to providing an inclusive and friendly environment for all, regardless of gender identity, sexual orientation, disability, physical appearance, body size, race, or religion.

📢 Reporting Issues

If someone makes you or anyone else feel unsafe or unwelcome, please report it as soon as possible to a staff member or email info@pythonforgood.org. All reports will be handled with discretion.

⚠️ Consequences

Participants violating these rules may be asked to leave the event without a refund, and future participation may be affected depending on the situation.

🙏 Acknowledgments

Our Code of Conduct is inspired by and adapted from the Contributor Covenant and other open source community standards.
